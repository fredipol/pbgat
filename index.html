What is this tool?

PBGAT (Preference-based Group Assignment Tool) helps teachers organise children into groups using the preferences collected from their class.

It uses the children’s ranked choices and the group sizes supplied by the user to suggest groupings. Vetoes and avoiding single-person groups are firm rules. Groups are single-sex by default, but the user can allow mixed-sex groups. The tool then aims to give as many children as possible at least one of their choices, with higher-ranked choices preferred.

The tool proposes a grouping and reports how well it matched the children’s choices. Some preferences may not be possible to satisfy together, so users should review the proposed groups before using them.

User instructions

Choose how to prepare your CSV

You can either use the Google Form template to collect responses, or fill in the downloadable CSV template yourself. Both options produce a file to upload on the PBGAT start page.

Option 1: Use the Google Form template

  1. Open the Google Form template folder.
  2. Make your own copy of the read-only form template in your Google Drive, then share your copy with pupils to collect responses.
  3. Open the response spreadsheet linked to your copied form. Add any teacher-entered vetoes in the Veto 1 and Veto 2 columns; pupils should not fill these fields.
  4. In Google Sheets, choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv).
  5. Return to PBGAT and select the downloaded CSV file.

Option 2: Fill in the CSV template yourself

  1. Download the CSV template and open it in a spreadsheet app, such as Google Sheets.
  2. Delete the fictional example row and enter one row per pupil. Keep the header row and column names unchanged.
  3. Enter each pupil’s five preferred group members in rank order. Choices must refer to pupils included in the respondent list.
  4. Leave veto columns blank unless a teacher needs to record a veto. Vetoes must be entered by staff, not pupils.
  5. Download or save the completed sheet as a comma-separated values (.csv) file, then select that file on the PBGAT start page.

Choose group settings

After reviewing the CSV, enter the number of groups and their maximum sizes. Single-sex groupings only is checked by default; untick it if mixed-sex groups are acceptable. Vetoes and the minimum group size of two remain in force either way.

Data protection

The CSV may contain children’s personal information. PBGAT reads and processes the file in your browser to check the responses and create proposed groupings. The app does not upload the CSV or results to its website or send them to a PBGAT server.

The app does not create an account or save the CSV in a PBGAT database. Information is held temporarily in the open browser page while you use the tool. Any files you download, copies kept elsewhere, and information in the original Google Sheet remain subject to your school’s usual handling and retention rules.

Your school or other responsible organisation remains responsible for deciding and documenting the lawful basis and purpose for using this information, providing appropriate privacy information to pupils and families, and applying its security, access, retention, and deletion policies. This short notice describes PBGAT’s handling only; it does not replace your organisation’s privacy notice or legal advice. Contact your school’s data protection lead if you are unsure whether you may use the data.
